Golden Rules for Using All 3 Together

Rule 1: KLSE MSS Daily is the gatekeeper — if it's NEUTRAL or EXIT, the other tools don't matter. Don't look at the 15M Sniper until Daily approves.


Rule 2: The 4H is your early warning system — it will show EXIT-AVOID BEFORE the daily deteriorates. Always check 4H when considering entry.
