## KLSE Macro Drivers (Priority Order)

### 1. US Federal Reserve (Most Important)
- Fed rate hikes → USD strengthens → MYR weakens → foreign funds exit KLSE
- Fed rate cuts → USD weakens → MYR strengthens → foreign funds return to EM
- **Watch**: FOMC meeting dates (8 per year)

### 2. BNM Overnight Policy Rate (OPR)
- BNM raises OPR → banks profit more (NIM widens) → **BUY banks**
- BNM raises OPR → REITs become less attractive → **SELL REITs**
- **Watch**: BNM Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) — 6 meetings per year

### 3. USD/MYR Exchange Rate
- MYR weakens (higher USD/MYR) → exporters benefit: tech, gloves, plantation
- MYR strengthens → importers benefit: consumer, airlines, retailers
- **Key level**: USD/MYR 4.0–4.5 range is "normal". Extreme moves signal stress.

### 4. Crude Palm Oil (CPO) Price
- CPO > RM4,000/tonne → plantation stocks rally (KLK, IOICORP, SDGUTHRIE)
- CPO < RM3,000/tonne → plantation stocks weak
- **Watch**: MPOB monthly supply/demand report, Indonesia export policy

### 5. Brent Crude Oil Price
- Higher oil → MISC, Dialog, Yinson, O&G sector rally
- Lower oil → Petronas reduces capex → O&G contractors suffer
- TENAGA (electricity) also affected by gas input costs

### 6. China / Global Manufacturing PMI
- KLSE tech sector (INARI, VITROX, MPI) tracks US/China semiconductor cycle
- Global PMI > 50 = expansion → tech stocks rally
- **Watch**: Caixin China Manufacturing PMI (monthly)

### 7. Government Budget & Projects
- Annual Budget (usually October/November) drives construction & infrastructure
- MRT, LRT, Pan Borneo Highway → GAMUDA, IJM, SUNCON, KERJAYA

## McClellan Oscillator for KLSE Breadth

The McClellan Oscillator measures market breadth (how many stocks are participating).

**Formula:**
```
Advancing issues  = stocks closing higher than previous day
Declining issues  = stocks closing lower than previous day
Net Advances      = Advancing – Declining
McClellan Osc     = EMA19(Net Advances) – EMA39(Net Advances)
McClellan Summation = Running sum of McClellan Oscillator
```

**Interpretation:**
| Signal                         | Meaning                                |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| McClellan Osc > 0              | Breadth expanding — market healthy     |
| McClellan Osc < 0              | Breadth contracting — watch out        |
| Osc crosses above 0 from below | Buy signal — broad rally starting      |
| Osc crosses below 0 from above | Sell signal — rally narrowing          |
| Summation > +500               | Overbought — reduce new longs          |
| Summation < –500               | Oversold — potential bottoming         |

## KLSE Trading Calendar — Key Annual Events

| Period          | Event                              | Market Impact                           |
|-----------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Jan             | Window dressing reversal           | Stocks that rose in Dec often retreat   |
| Feb             | CNY — reduced trading volume       | Thin markets, be cautious               |
| Mar–Apr         | Q4 results season                  | Earnings surprises drive moves          |
| May             | "Sell in May" seasonal bias        | Global risk-off — watch KLCI support    |
| Jun             | KLCI/MID70 semi-annual review      | Index additions rally, removals fall    |
| Jul–Aug         | Q1 results, Eid al-Adha            | Retail light, watch foreign flows       |
| Sep             | Fed Sept meeting (often key)       | USD/MYR sensitive period               |
| Oct             | Budget announcement                | Construction/property spike pre-Budget |
| Nov             | US election years (every 4 yrs)    | Global volatility                       |
| Dec             | KLCI/MID70 second review           | Window dressing — index stocks rally    |

## Foreign Fund Flow Tracking

Bursa publishes **daily foreign buy/sell** data:
- Net foreign buy > RM200M/day = strong inflow — bullish
- Net foreign sell > RM200M/day = outflow pressure — bearish
- Sustained net sell for 5+ days = caution on large caps

Check at: [Bursa Market Stats](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market/market_data/market_statistics) or klsescreener.com
