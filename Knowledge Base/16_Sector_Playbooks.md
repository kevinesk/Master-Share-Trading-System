## 1. Banking Sector

### Key Stocks
| Stock | Code | Role |
|-------|------|------|
| MAYBANK | 1155 | Largest bank; bellwether |
| CIMB | 1023 | Most liquid, follows Maybank |
| PBBANK | 1295 | Highest quality metrics (ROE) |
| RHBBANK | 1066 | More volatile; higher beta |
| HLFG | 5819 | Hong Leong Group; conservative |
| AMBANK | 1015 | Smaller; M&A speculation history |

### What Drives Banking Stocks

**Primary driver: BNM Overnight Policy Rate (OPR)**
- OPR hike → Net Interest Margin (NIM) expands → Earnings rise → Stock price rises
- OPR cut → NIM compresses → Earnings fall → Stock price falls
- **Lead time**: Bank stocks often price in OPR changes 1–2 months before BNM announces

**Secondary drivers**:
- **Loan growth**: Consumer + business loan growth drives revenue
- **Asset quality**: Non-performing loans (NPL ratio < 1.5% = healthy)
- **Dividend yield**: Malaysian banks pay 4–7% DY; acts as floor in downturns
- **Foreign flows**: Banks are the largest KLCI component; foreign fund rotation heavily impacts price

### Best Time to Buy Banking Stocks
- BNM signals OPR hike or holds rates at elevated level
- Post-earnings if results beat expectations and NPL is stable
- After KLCI correction to EMA50 support (banks lead the KLCI recovery)
- When USD/MYR weakens (foreign funds buying MYR assets → banks first)

### Banking Sector Metrics to Track
| Metric | Healthy | Warning |
|--------|---------|---------|
| NIM | >2.2% | <1.8% |
| NPL Ratio | <1.5% | >2.5% |
| ROE | >10% | <8% |
| Loan Growth | >5% YoY | <3% or negative |
| CET1 Ratio | >13% | <11% |

### Trading Approach
- **Position trade**: Buy MAYBANK or CIMB at sector dips; hold for dividend + capital appreciation
- **Swing trade**: RHBBANK and AMBANK are more volatile; better for 15–30% swing targets
- **Stop**: Below EMA50 on weekly chart for position trades; below VCP low for swing trades

## 2. Technology / Semiconductor Sector

### Key Stocks
| Stock | Code | Specialisation |
|-------|------|---------------|
| VITROX | 0097 | Vision inspection systems — high growth |
| FRONTKN | 0128 | Semiconductor equipment cleaning |
| INARI | 0166 | RF components (Apple supply chain) |
| UNISEM | 5005 | Semiconductor assembly and test |
| MPI | 3867 | Semiconductor packaging |
| GREATEC | 0208 | Equipment for semiconductor |
| UWC | 5292 | Precision machining for semiconductor |

### What Drives Tech/Semi Stocks

**Primary driver: Global semiconductor cycle**
- US SOX Index (Philadelphia Semiconductor Index) leads KLSE tech stocks by 2–6 weeks
- When SOX is in uptrend → KLSE semiconductor stocks outperform
- Key reference: `NASDAQ:SOX` on TradingView

**Secondary drivers**:
- **USD/MYR exchange rate**: Most revenue in USD; weaker MYR = higher MYR earnings
- **AI/data centre demand**: NVIDIA/AMD capex drives demand for Malaysian chip packaging/testing
- **Apple product cycle**: INARI revenue tied to Apple iPhone production cycle (Sep–Nov = peak)
- **China semiconductor policy**: US-China chip restrictions can redirect orders to Malaysian fabs

### Best Time to Buy
- After a significant sector correction (SOX pulls back 15–20%) and then turns up
- When USD strengthens against MYR (earnings boost)
- When major US chip companies guide for inventory recovery (bottom of semi cycle)
- Post-earnings if results beat and management guides positively for next quarter

### Semiconductor Cycle Timeline
```
Semi cycle is approximately 2.5–3 years:
Downturn → Bottom → Recovery → Expansion → Peak → Downturn
              ↑                      ↑
           Buy here               Take profits
```

**Signs of cycle bottom** (buy signal):
- Inventory corrections at US chip companies ending
- Book-to-bill ratio rising above 1.0
- SOX making 52-week lows on low volume, then recovering

**Signs of cycle peak** (sell signal):
- SOX making parabolic new highs
- Lead times for chip orders shortening (demand weakening)
- Malaysian semi stocks showing high PE >30× on forward earnings

### Trading Approach
- **High beta**: VITROX and GREATEC can move ±30% in weeks
- **Use warrants cautiously**: High volatility = expensive warrants; premium often >20%
- **VCP setups work well**: Tech stocks form beautiful bases during semi downcycles
- **Key watch**: INARI results (March, June, September, December) — guides sector

## 3. Plantation Sector

### Key Stocks
| Stock | Code | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| KLK | 2445 | Premium quality; downstream exposure |
| IOICORP | 1961 | Integrated; oleochemicals |
| SDGUTHRIE | 5285 | Pure upstream plantation |
| PPB | 4065 | Diversified (Wilmar stake + cinema) |
| GENP | 2291 | Pure upstream; volatile |
| UTDPLT | 2089 | Small but quality |
| SOP | 5126 | Sarawak-focused |
| FFB | 5306 | High-beta pure planter |

### What Drives Plantation Stocks

**Primary driver: CPO (Crude Palm Oil) price**
- CPO price is set in Bursa Malaysia's derivatives market (FCPO futures)
- CPO >RM4,000/tonne → plantation stocks very profitable
- CPO <RM3,000/tonne → most planters at breakeven or loss
- Check: `BURSA:FCPO1!` or `MYX:PALMOIL` on TradingView

**Secondary drivers**:
| Driver | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Soybean oil price (CBOT) | Substitute; higher soy = higher palm demand/price |
| Biodiesel mandates (B30, B35) | Increases domestic CPO consumption |
| Indonesia export policy | Indonesia (60% of global supply) policies move CPO price |
| Weather (La Niña/El Niño) | Affects FFB (fresh fruit bunch) yield — La Niña = lower yields |
| USD/MYR | CPO priced in MYR; but export demand from China/India in USD |

### Best Time to Buy
- CPO price recovering from below RM3,000 back toward RM4,000
- El Niño weather event confirmed (lowers yield → tighter supply → higher price)
- Indonesia raises export tax on CPO (reduces competition → Malaysia CPO benefits)
- China importing more CPO (demand surge)

### Plantation Metrics
| Metric | Healthy | Concern |
|--------|---------|---------|
| CPO price | >RM3,500/tonne | <RM2,800/tonne |
| FFB yield | >20 MT/hectare | <15 MT/hectare |
| Cost of production | <RM2,500/tonne | >RM3,000/tonne |
| Debt/Equity | <0.5× | >1.0× |

### Trading Approach
- Plantation stocks are **cyclical** — buy low in the cycle, sell near the top
- CPO chart is the primary chart to watch, not the stock chart
- Plantation stocks move together — diversifying within the sector provides little benefit
- **Best setup**: VCP formation after CPO price recovery and sector lags

### What Drives Property Stocks

**Primary drivers**:
1. **BNM OPR** (interest rates): Lower rates → cheaper mortgages → higher demand → higher prices
2. **Sales GDV**: Gross Development Value of new launches — tracks new bookings
3. **Government policy**: HOC (Home Ownership Campaign), affordable housing quotas, stamp duty exemptions
4. **Iskandar/JB development**: Johor-Singapore RTS Link, Special Financial Zone → huge catalyst for Johor property

**The Johor Catalyst (2024–2026)**:
- Johor-Singapore Special Financial Zone (SFZ) — direct catalyst for UEMS, KSL, TROP, ECOWLD
- RTS Link (Johor Bahru-Singapore rapid transit) — opens 2026
- Data centres in Johor by Google, Microsoft, Oracle → high-paying jobs → property demand
- **Key stocks to watch**: UEMS, KSL, TROP, ECOWLD

### Best Time to Buy Property Stocks
- OPR cut cycle beginning (lower mortgage rates boost affordability)
- New major development announcements (budget, state policies)
- After sector-wide correction when fundamentals remain sound
- When unbilled sales (future revenue) are rising

### Property Metrics
| Metric | Healthy | Concern |

| Net gearing | <0.5× | >1.0× |

### What Drives Healthcare
- **Medical tourism**: IHH and KPJ benefit from medical tourists (India, Middle East, Indonesia)
- **Ageing population**: Malaysia's demographic shift increases healthcare demand
- **Gloves**: Driven by global healthcare spending and pandemic/post-pandemic restocking cycles
- **Government policy**: Privatisation of government hospitals (opportunity for IHH/KPJ)

### Glove Sector — Special Note
- COVID (2020–2021): TOPGLOV and KOSSAN went up 400–800% on unprecedented demand
- Post-COVID (2022–2023): Glove sector crashed 80–90% on excess inventory and oversupply
- Now: Normalising — watch for inventory clearance and return to sustainable demand
- **Do not buy glove stocks in a downtrend** — they can fall further than you expect
- **Buy when**: Inventory normalises + ASP (average selling price) starts recovering

## 6. REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)

### Key KLSE REITs
| Stock | Code | Asset Type |
|-------|------|-----------|
| SUNREIT | 5176 | Sunway Pyramid + hotels |
| PAVREIT | 5212 | Pavilion KL (premium mall) |
| IGBREIT | 5227 | Mid Valley Megamall |
| AXREIT | 5106 | Industrial / logistics properties |

### What Drives REITs
- **BNM OPR**: REITs are bond proxies. Higher rates → REITs less attractive vs bonds. Lower rates → REITs rally.
- **Retail footfall**: Mall REITs depend on retail spending and occupancy rates
- **Distribution Yield**: REITs must distribute 90% of income. DY of 5–8% is typical.

### REIT Trading Rules
- REITs are more suitable for **income investing** than swing trading
- But REIT prices DO move: buy when yield is high (price depressed), sell when yield is low (price elevated)
- **Actionable yield levels**:
  - DY > 7% on quality REITs = buy (price is depressed)
  - DY < 4% = overvalued; take profits

## Sector Rotation Calendar for KLSE

```
Interest Rate Cycle → Sector Performance:

OPR Rising:
  Beneficiaries: Banking (NIM expansion), Insurance
  Losers: REITs, Property, Utilities (interest-sensitive)

OPR Falling:
  Beneficiaries: REITs, Property, Utilities
  Losers: Banking (NIM compression)

USD Strong (MYR weak):
  Beneficiaries: Technology/Semi (USD revenues), Plantation (CPO exports)
  Losers: Airlines, Importers, Utilities (USD costs)

USD Weak (MYR strong):
  Beneficiaries: Airlines, Importers, Retailers
  Losers: Exporters (Tech, Plantation)

Global Risk-On (US rally):
  Beneficiaries: Technology, Small/mid-cap growth
  Losers: Defensives (Utilities, Consumer staples)

Global Risk-Off:
  Beneficiaries: Utilities, Consumer Staples, Telcos, Gold
  Losers: Banks, Technology, Property
```
