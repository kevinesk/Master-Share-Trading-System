## ⚠️ Quality Trap Warnings

Even high Q-score names can be traps. Watch for:

1. **Stage 4 downtrend** — quality compounder in a falling chart = wait
2. **Sector headwind** — Q-A bank in a rate-hike cycle = avoid timing
3. **Insider selling** — directors dumping = red flag despite Q-score
4. **Auditor change** — sudden auditor switch = governance risk
5. **PN17/GN3 status** — Bursa-flagged distress = automatic Q-D
6. **One-off earnings boost** — disposal gain inflates ROE = adjust
7. **Aggressive accounting** — large receivables vs sales = revenue manipulation
8. **High insider sell-down** within last 6 months — disagree with own thesis

If 2+ red flags → downgrade by one full grade.
