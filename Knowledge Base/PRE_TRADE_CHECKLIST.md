# Pre-Trade Checklist — 12 Gates
**Status**: MANDATORY before every BUY order. Print this. Tape to monitor.
**Purpose**: Catch the mistake BEFORE it costs you money.

> **The rule**: ALL 12 gates must be 🟢 GREEN. Even one 🔴 RED = NO TRADE.

---

## 🐋 WHALE INVESTOR PRE-BUY GATE (Adam Khoo VMI™ 7-Step)

**Run this FIRST and ONLY if the trade is tagged "Whale / investing bucket"** (months-to-years hold, fundamental + technical hybrid). For swing/intraday trades, skip directly to Gate 1.

If passing this gate, you still proceed through all 12 gates below. This is a **pre-qualifier**, not a replacement.

| # | Khoo VMI Check | Pass Criterion | ✅/❌ |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Wonderful business with moat | KLSE Quality Score ≥ 35/50 | ☐ |
| W2 | Consistent earnings growth | EPS up in 5 of last 7 years | ☐ |
| W3 | Strong ROE | ROE ≥ 15% for 3+ consecutive years | ☐ |
| W4 | Low debt | D/E ≤ 0.5 (or net cash position) | ☐ |
| W5 | Trading below intrinsic value | ≥20% discount on 3 of 4 valuation methods (PEG / DCF / Discounted Earnings / P/B) | ☐ |
| W6 | LT uptrend confirmed | Price > 200 EMA AND EMA50 > EMA200 (weekly) | ☐ |
| W7 | Momentum entry trigger | Bounce off EMA50 OR base breakout on volume ≥1.5× avg | ☐ |

**Pass = ≥6 of 7.**

**🔴 STOP** (do not promote to Whale bucket) if Whale score < 6/7. Either re-tag as a swing trade (run all 12 gates below as swing rules), or skip entirely.

**🐋 Khoo Whale exit triggers** (write into journal at entry — exit if ANY fire later):
1. Fundamentals deteriorate: 2 consecutive quarters of EPS decline
2. Price closes below 200 EMA weekly AND holds for 4 weeks
3. Original thesis broken (regulatory shock, moat erosion, accounting concern)
4. Materially better VMI candidate appears (relative replacement)

## ⛔ Gate 1 — Market Regime (Marks + Weinstein)

- [ ] KLCI is ABOVE its 200-day MA
- [ ] KLCI 50-day MA > 200-day MA (golden cross intact)
- [ ] KLCI made a higher high within the last 4 weeks
- [ ] Marks cycle score (1-10): I rate it at ____ (must be 3-7; not <3 or >8)

**🔴 STOP if KLCI is in Stage 4 (declining trend). No long-only trades in bear regime.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 2 — Sector Health (Stovall + Weinstein + Morris)

Stock sector: __________ (Banking / Tech / Property / Construction / Plantation / Healthcare / etc.)

- [ ] Sector index is ABOVE its 30-week MA
- [ ] Sector 30W MA is FLAT or RISING (not falling)
- [ ] % of stocks in sector above 50-day MA ≥ 60%
- [ ] Sector A-D line not making lower lows
- [ ] Stock's sector is favored by current cycle phase (check Stovall map)

**🔴 STOP if sector is in Stage 4 or breadth deteriorating.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 3 — Top-Down Macro (Pauline Yong + Dalio)

- [ ] OPR trajectory does not contradict thesis (rate-sensitive stocks aligned with cycle)
- [ ] MYR direction does not contradict thesis (exporters vs importers logic)
- [ ] No major event in next 5 trading days (Budget, OPR decision, election)
- [ ] EPF/PNB flow direction supportive (or neutral)

**🔴 STOP if macro is squarely against the trade (e.g., buying property on rising-rate cycle).**

---

## ⛔ Gate 4 — Trend Template (Minervini 8/8)

- [ ] Price > 50-day MA
- [ ] Price > 150-day MA
- [ ] Price > 200-day MA
- [ ] 50-day MA > 150-day MA
- [ ] 150-day MA > 200-day MA
- [ ] 200-day MA trending UP for ≥1 month
- [ ] Price ≥30% above 52-week low
- [ ] Price within 25% of 52-week high
- [ ] RS rating ≥ 70 (vs KLCI peers)

**Score: __ / 8**
**🔴 STOP if score < 7/8 for momentum trades. Minimum 6/8 for value/yield trades.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 5 — Base / Pattern Quality (O'Neil + Minervini + Darvas)

- [ ] Base is at least 5-7 weeks long (proper cup, flat, or VCP)
- [ ] No more than 2-3 prior bases stacked (avoid late-stage 4th+ base)
- [ ] Right side of base built on lighter volume than left side
- [ ] Pivot point identified at RM ______
- [ ] Volume contraction visible (Pocket Pivot / VCP dryup)
- [ ] No major selling spike (>2x avg vol red day) in last 5 days

**🔴 STOP if no clear pivot or 4th+ stage base.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 6 — Volume Confirmation (O'Neil + Fred Tam)

- [ ] Recent contraction days show below-average volume
- [ ] Today's breakout candle (if triggered) is ≥1.5x 50-day avg volume
- [ ] Accumulation days (up + volume) outnumber distribution days (down + volume) in last 4 weeks
- [ ] No churn (high volume + flat price) in last 3 days

**🔴 STOP if breakout volume < 1.2x average — fakeout risk.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 7 — Entry Trigger (Specific, Not Vague)

- [ ] My entry price is: RM ______
- [ ] My trigger is: (Pivot break / Pullback to 10D EMA / Cheat entry — circle one)
- [ ] Price is within 1% of trigger (no chasing beyond 1% extension)
- [ ] Order type planned: Limit @ RM ______
- [ ] I will NOT buy if price gaps up >2% above trigger (chase risk)

**🔴 STOP if I'm 'kind of close' to the trigger but not at it.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 8 — Stop Loss Predefined

- [ ] My stop price is: RM ______
- [ ] Stop placement reason: (Below 50 EMA / Below base low / Below pivot / -7% rule)
- [ ] **ATR offset applied**: stop is placed **0.5–1 × ATR(14) BELOW** the obvious swing low / round number / pivot — NOT at it. ATR(14) = ______ RM, offset used = ______ RM (PAM anti-stop-hunt rule)
- [ ] Stop distance from entry: ____ % (must be ≤8%)
- [ ] Stop will be PLACED IN BROKER immediately after fill (not mental)
- [ ] I accept this loss size in MYR: RM ______

**🔴 STOP if no stop is defined OR distance >8% OR stop sits AT an obvious round number / visible swing low (stop-hunt target).**

**Why the ATR offset**: institutional players sweep stops sitting at the obvious swing low / round number before reversing the move. Placing the stop 0.5–1 ATR below the obvious level keeps you in the trade through the sweep. Trade-off: slightly wider loss if the stop does fire — which is why position size must be re-calculated AFTER the offset is applied (use the wider stop distance in the 1% risk formula). ## ⛔ Gate 9 — Position Sizing (10% Hard Cap)

- [ ] Portfolio current value: RM ______
- [ ] Max units (10% cap) = (Portfolio × 0.10) ÷ Entry = ______ → rounded 100s: ______
- [ ] Target units (7%) = ______
- [ ] Risk-based units (1% rule) = (Portfolio × 0.01) ÷ Stop distance = ______
- [ ] **Final size = MIN of (Target, Risk-based, Max) = ______ units**
- [ ] Final cost = Size × Entry = RM ______
- [ ] Final % of portfolio = ______ % (MUST be ≤ 10%)

**🔴 STOP if calculated size > 10% of portfolio. No exceptions.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 10 — Profit Targets Defined

- [ ] T1 (first tranche, sell 1/3): RM ______
- [ ] T2 (second tranche, sell 1/3): RM ______
- [ ] T3 (final tranche, trail): RM ______
- [ ] Reward-to-risk ratio (T2 ÷ stop distance): ____ : 1 (must be ≥ 2:1)

**🔴 STOP if R:R < 2:1. The math doesn't work over time.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 11 — Psychology / Anti-FOMO

- [ ] I planned this trade ≥1 day ago (not impulse today)
- [ ] I am NOT chasing because I "missed" yesterday's move
- [ ] I am NOT trying to recover a recent loss
- [ ] I am NOT trading because I'm bored / no setups today
- [ ] I have NOT taken any caffeine spike / no emotion peak right now
- [ ] If this trade hits stop, I will accept it without revenge-trading

**🔴 STOP if any FOMO / revenge / boredom signal. Step away 1 hour.**

---

## ⛔ Gate 12 — Portfolio Heat

- [ ] My current open positions count: ______ (max 6-10)
- [ ] My current total invested %: ______ (max 80% in equities)
- [ ] My current sector concentration: largest sector = ____ % (max 25%)
- [ ] My current open risk (sum of all stops): ______ % (max 5% portfolio at risk)

**🔴 STOP if portfolio heat > 5% or sector concentration > 25%.**

---

## 🟢 ALL GATES GREEN — Execute the Trade

When and ONLY when all 12 gates are ✅:

1. Place limit order at planned entry
2. After fill, immediately place stop loss order in broker
3. Log the trade in `TRADE_JOURNAL.md` with:
   - All 12 gate answers
   - Entry, stop, targets, size
   - Thesis in 2 sentences
4. Set price alerts at T1, T2, T3, stop
5. Walk away from the screen

---

## 🔴 ONE OR MORE GATES RED — No Trade

1. Note WHICH gate failed
2. Write it in journal under "Trades I Didn't Take"
3. Wait for next setup
4. Do NOT rationalize a 🔴 to 🟢 — that's how accounts die
