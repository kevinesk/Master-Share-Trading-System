# Technical Chart Patterns — KLSE Playbook

## The Core Truth
Patterns repeat because human psychology repeats. Fear and greed create the same shapes over and over across all markets, including KLSE.

**Pattern hierarchy**: Stage 2 trend first → then pattern → then volume confirmation → then entry.
Never trade a pattern in a Stage 3 or Stage 4 stock.

---

## Continuation Patterns (Trade WITH the trend)

### 1. Cup and Handle (Most Reliable)

```
        ___                     ___
       /   \                   /
      /     \       _____     /   ← Breakout here
     /       \     / hdl \   /
____/         \___/       \_/
              ^cup^  ^h^
```

**Structure**:
- Cup: Rounded bottom (U-shape, not V-shape) — 7 to 65 weeks
- Handle: Small pullback of 10–15% from cup lip, forms over 1–4 weeks
- Handle should drift DOWN on LOW volume (shakeout of weak holders)
- Breakout from handle on HEAVY volume (≥1.5× average)

**KLSE rules**:
- Cup depth: 15–33% from peak to low (deeper = riskier)
- Handle must be in upper half of cup
- Stock must be in Stage 2 before the cup forms
- Buy at: Handle high + 1 tick (pivot buy point)
- Stop: Below handle low (or 7–8% max)
- Target: Cup depth projected upward from breakout

**Ideal cup characteristics**:
| Feature | Ideal | Acceptable | Avoid |
|---------|-------|------------|-------|
| Shape | Smooth U | Slightly V | Sharp V |
| Depth | 15–25% | 26–33% | >35% |
| Duration | 7–26 weeks | 4–7 weeks | <3 weeks |
| Right side volume | Lower than left | Similar | Higher |

### 2. Bull Flag (Fastest Pattern — Intraday & Swing)

```
       ___
      /   |
     /    |  ← flag (slight downward drift on low volume)
    /     |
___/      |___  ← breakout
   pole      continuation
```

**Structure**:
- Pole: Sharp, near-vertical move up (5–15 days) on heavy volume
- Flag: Tight consolidation slanting slightly down, 5–15 days
- Volume: Dries up during flag, explodes on breakout
- Breakout: Above upper flag boundary

**KLSE rules**:
- Pole must be at least +15% in ≤10 trading days
- Flag retraces at most 38.2% of the pole (use Fibonacci)
- Flag duration: 3–15 trading days (longer = weaker)
- Buy: Upper flag trendline + volume confirmation
- Stop: Below lower flag trendline
- Target: Pole length measured from breakout point

**Quick check**: If the flag is deeper than 50% of the pole, it's no longer a flag — it's a pullback. Skip it.

---

### 3. Flat Base (Minervini's Favourite)

```
_________________________________________
                                         |
                                         |____ ← breakout
```

- Price moves sideways in a tight range (≤10–12% wide)
- Usually 5–10 weeks
- Occurs AFTER a prior uptrend (second-stage base)
- Volume contracts during base, explodes on breakout
- **KLSE advantage**: Very common in quality dividend stocks during sector rotation pauses

**Entry**: Above flat base high + volume surge
**Stop**: Below flat base low
**Why it works**: Sellers have exhausted themselves; any new buying tips the balance

---

### 4. Ascending Triangle

```
_______________________  ← flat resistance
   /\    /\    /\   /  ← breakout
  /  \  /  \  /  \/
 /    \/    \/
```

- Flat top (resistance), rising bottoms (higher lows)
- Duration: 3–8 weeks minimum
- Volume: Contracts as pattern forms, surge on breakout
- **Bullish** when price breaks above flat top on high volume
- Buy: Flat top + 1 tick, on volume ≥1.5× average
- Stop: Below most recent higher low
- Target: Triangle height projected from breakout

---

### 5. High Tight Flag (Explosive — rare and powerful)

- Stock gains ≥100% in 4–8 weeks (pole)
- Then consolidates 10–25% over 1–4 weeks (flag)
- **Most powerful pattern** — Minervini's highest-rated setup
- Very rare on KLSE; most common in tech/glove stocks post-earnings
- When it appears: Use full position sizing immediately

## Reversal Patterns (Signal trend change — use with caution)

### 6. Head and Shoulders (Bearish Reversal)

```
         ___
        /   \     ← head
   ___/     \___
  /   \     /   \  ← shoulders
_/     \___/     \___
       neckline
```

**Trading rule for KLSE**:
- Do NOT short on KLSE (not easily available for retail)
- Use it to EXIT long positions when right shoulder forms
- Neckline break = exit signal; do not wait to confirm
- Volume: Higher on left shoulder peak, lower on head peak, very low on right shoulder peak

**Measured target** (if market allows short selling):
- Distance from head to neckline, projected DOWN from neckline break

### 7. Double Bottom (W Pattern — Bullish Reversal)

```
        ___
       /   \        /
      /     \      /  ← breakout above neckline
     /       \____/
```

- Two lows at roughly same price level
- Second low should have LOWER volume than first (selling exhaustion)
- Neckline = peak between the two lows
- Buy: Close above neckline on high volume
- Stop: Below second low
- Target: Neckline-to-bottom distance projected upward

**KLSE note**: Common in cyclical stocks (plantation, banking) at sector bottoms.

## Candlestick Patterns (Short-term signals — confirm with other indicators)

### Bullish Reversal Candles

| Pattern | Description | Reliability | Best Used When |
|---------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Hammer | Small body, long lower wick, at support | High | After downtrend, at support/EMA |
| Bullish Engulfing | Green candle fully engulfs prior red | High | At support, after 3+ down days |
| Morning Star | 3 candles: down, small, large up | High | At major support levels |
| Doji | Open = Close, indecision | Medium | At support after downtrend |
| Pin Bar | Thin body, long lower wick | Medium | At Fibonacci levels |

### Bearish Warning Candles

| Pattern | Description | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Shooting Star | Small body, long upper wick | Consider tightening stop |
| Bearish Engulfing | Red candle engulfs prior green | Alert: thesis may be changing |
| Evening Star | 3 candles: up, small, large down | Exit if at resistance |
| Gravestone Doji | Open = Close at low of day | Exit signal near resistance |

## Volume Rules for ALL Patterns

**Breakout must have**:
- Volume ≥ 1.5× 20-day average volume (minimum)
- Volume ≥ 2× average = high-conviction breakout
- Volume < 1.5× average = suspect breakout — wait for confirmation

**During base formation**:
- Weeks with high volume → price should NOT fall (accumulation)
- Weeks with low volume → price can drift sideways or down (shakeout)
- Trend: Volume should DRY UP as base matures (smart money done selling)

---

## Pattern Failure Rules

Exit if:
1. Price closes **below pivot buy point** on heavy volume (failed breakout)
2. Price re-enters the base after breakout (buyback risk)
3. Market (KLCI) breaks below EMA50 while pattern forms
4. Volume on the breakout day is below average

**Failed breakout action**: Exit immediately. Do not wait. Failed breakouts often lead to -15% to -30% drops in days.

## Quick Pattern Checklist (Before Every Entry)

- [ ] Stock is in Stage 2 uptrend (EMA50 > EMA150 > EMA200)?
- [ ] Pattern is clearly identifiable (not squinting to see it)?
- [ ] Base depth is acceptable (≤33%)?
- [ ] Volume contracted during base formation?
- [ ] Breakout volume is ≥1.5× average?
- [ ] Entry is within 2–3% of pivot buy point?
- [ ] Stop loss is defined and acceptable (<8% from entry)?
- [ ] Risk:reward is at least 1:2?
