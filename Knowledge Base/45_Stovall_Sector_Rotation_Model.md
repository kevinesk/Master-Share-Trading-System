# Sam Stovall — Sector Rotation Model

> "The market is forward-looking; it discounts the economy 6 months in advance. Sector leadership rotates before the economic data confirms the cycle phase."
> — Sam Stovall, *Sector Investing*

Sam Stovall (Chief Investment Strategist, CFRA Research) formalised the relationship between **economic cycle phases** and **sector leadership** in his 1996 book and decades of CFRA research. His framework gives you a roadmap: based on where the economy is, you know which sectors should lead.

This file applies his model to the KLSE economy and Bursa sectors.

---

## Part 1 — The Core Insight

Different sectors thrive in different economic phases because their EARNINGS respond differently to interest rates, consumer behaviour, and credit availability.

The economy cycles through 4 phases. Each phase has consistent sector winners and losers, observed across decades of data.

```
                Full Expansion (peak growth)
                  ↗                    ↘
   Early Recovery                       Early Recession
   (growth accelerating)                (growth decelerating)
                  ↖                    ↙
                Full Recession (trough)
```

**The trader's edge**: identify the current phase, position into the LEADING sectors, exit the LAGGING sectors. The cycle moves slowly (each phase typically lasts 6-18 months), giving you time to act.

## Part 2 — The 4 Economic Phases (Detailed)

### Phase 1 — Full Recession (The Trough)

**Economic signals**:
- GDP contracting (negative growth)
- Unemployment rising sharply
- BNM (or central bank) cutting rates aggressively
- Inflation falling
- Bond yields falling (flight to safety)
- Consumer confidence at lows

**KLSE signals**:
- KLCI down 25%+ from prior peak
- McClellan Oscillator deeply negative for weeks
- Foreign selling persistent
- USD/MYR strengthening (safe-haven flows out of MYR)
- Penny stock activity dead

**Leading sectors in Phase 1**:
1. **Bonds (not sector, but for context)** — flight to safety
2. **Consumer Staples** — defensive consumption
3. **Utilities** — regulated returns, dividend
4. **Healthcare (non-cyclical)** — demand inelastic

**KLSE Phase 1 winners**:
- NESTLE, F&N (staples)
- TENAGA, GAS MALAYSIA (utilities)
- IHH, KPJ (healthcare)
- REITs (yield, defensive)

### Phase 2 — Early Recovery (The Spring)

**Economic signals**:
- GDP growth turning positive
- BNM/central bank rates at lows; starting to hold
- Credit availability returning
- Consumer confidence rising from lows
- Bond yields starting to rise (anticipating future rate hikes)

**KLSE signals**:
- KLCI breaking above 50-day MA for first time in months
- McClellan Oscillator crossing back above 0
- Volume returning
- Foreign buying resuming
- USD/MYR stabilising

**Leading sectors in Phase 2** (the highest-return phase!):
1. **Consumer Discretionary** — pent-up demand released
2. **Technology** — corporate investment resumes
3. **Financials (Banks)** — credit growth resumes
4. **Industrials** — capex pickup
5. **Real Estate / Construction** — interest rates still low

**KLSE Phase 2 winners**:
- AEON, MR DIY (consumer discretionary)
- VITROX, INARI, FRONTKN (tech)
- MAYBANK, CIMB (banks)
- GAMUDA, IJM (industrials/construction)
- SIMEPROP, MAHSING (property)

### Phase 3 — Full Expansion (The Summer)

**Economic signals**:
- GDP growth strong (above trend)
- Unemployment at low levels
- BNM/central bank raising rates
- Inflation rising
- Bond yields elevated
- Consumer confidence strong

**KLSE signals**:
- KLCI making new highs
- Breadth healthy (many stocks at new highs)
- Foreign positioning long-heavy
- USD/MYR stable to weakening (capital inflows)
- Mid/small-cap participation broadening

**Leading sectors in Phase 3**:
1. **Energy** — late-cycle commodity demand peak
2. **Materials / Plantation** — commodity prices peaking
3. **Industrials** — full capacity utilisation
4. **Technology (mature)** — continued growth but pricing in late-cycle

**KLSE Phase 3 winners**:
- PETDAG, PETRONAS CHEMICALS (energy)
- KLK, IOI, SIME PLANTATION (plantation)
- PCHEM, LCT (chemicals/materials)

### Phase 4 — Early Recession (The Autumn)

**Economic signals**:
- GDP growth slowing
- BNM/central bank holding or cutting rates
- Inflation peaking and starting to fall
- Bond yields falling (recession anticipation)
- Consumer confidence weakening
- Layoffs beginning

**KLSE signals**:
- KLCI making lower highs
- Breadth deteriorating
- Foreign selling beginning
- USD/MYR strengthening (risk-off)
- Defensive sectors outperforming

**Leading sectors in Phase 4**:
1. **Consumer Staples** — defensive rotation
2. **Healthcare** — defensive rotation
3. **Utilities** — defensive rotation
4. **Telecoms** — defensive rotation
5. **Gold / precious metals** — safe-haven flows

**KLSE Phase 4 winners**:
- NESTLE, F&N (staples)
- IHH, KPJ (healthcare)
- TENAGA, YTL POWER (utilities)
- MAXIS, CDB (telco)
- Gold-linked plays

```
                    PHASE 3 — Full Expansion
                    Late-cycle sectors lead:
                    • Energy
                    • Materials/Plantation
                    • Industrials
                  ╱                          ╲
                ╱                              ╲
              ╱                                  ╲
   PHASE 2 — Early Recovery              PHASE 4 — Early Recession
   Cyclical sectors lead:               Defensive sectors lead:
   • Consumer Discretionary             • Consumer Staples
   • Tech                                • Healthcare
   • Financials                          • Utilities
   • Industrials                         • Telecoms
              ╲                                  ╱
                ╲                              ╱
                  ╲                          ╱
                    PHASE 1 — Full Recession
                    Bond proxies lead:
                    • REITs (yield)
                    • Utilities
                    • Staples
                    • Gold
```

### How to Use the Wheel

1. Identify current phase (Part 4 method)
2. Rotate INTO the leading sectors for the current phase
3. Begin reducing exposure to the leading sectors of the PREVIOUS phase (they peak as the phase ends)
4. Be neutral on the LAGGING sectors for the current phase

**Critical timing**: The market rotates BEFORE the economic data confirms the phase. By the time GDP data confirms "we're in early recovery," tech and financials may already be up 30%.

## Part 4 — How to Identify the Current Phase

### Method 1: The Yield Curve

The yield curve (10-year minus 2-year Treasury yield) is the most reliable phase indicator.

| Yield Curve | Phase |
|------------|-------|
| Steeply positive (>1.5%) | Phase 2 — Early Recovery |
| Mildly positive (0.5-1.5%) | Phase 3 — Full Expansion (early) |
| Flat (0-0.5%) | Phase 3 — Full Expansion (late) |
| Inverted (negative) | Phase 4 — Early Recession warning |
| Re-steepening after inversion | Phase 1 — Recession trough |

**KLSE proxy**: Use Malaysian Government Securities (MGS) 10-year minus 3-year yield.

### Method 2: BNM Rate Cycle Position

| BNM OPR Position | Likely Phase |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Cutting aggressively | Phase 1 |
| Cuts ending, rates at low | Phase 2 |
| Hiking | Phase 3 |
| Hikes ending, considering cuts | Phase 4 |

### Method 3: The Economic Indicators Quick-Check

| Indicator | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 | Phase 4 |
|-----------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| GDP growth | < 0% | 1-3% | 3-5% | 2-3% slowing |
| Inflation | Falling | Low, rising | Rising fast | Peaking |
| Unemployment | High, peaking | High, falling | Low | Low, rising |
| Consumer confidence | Low | Recovering | High | Falling |
| Auto sales | Low | Recovering | Peak | Falling |
| Housing starts | Low | Rising | Peak | Falling |

### Method 4: The Sector Behaviour Check (Confirmation)

The cleanest validation: are the right sectors actually leading?

- If Tech + Banks are leading → Phase 2 confirmed
- If Energy + Materials are leading → Phase 3 confirmed
- If Staples + Utilities + Healthcare are leading → Phase 1 or 4
- If REITs + Gold are leading → Late Phase 4 / Early Phase 1

When 2 of 4 methods agree, you have high conviction on the phase.

### KLSE-Specific Quirks (Where Stovall's Model Adapts)

1. **Banks act earlier than US banks** — KLSE banks lead recovery very strongly because they're highly leveraged to domestic credit cycle. Phase 2 banks = best trade.

2. **Plantation has its OWN cycle** — CPO supply/demand cycle (4-7 years) can diverge from broader economic cycle. Sometimes plantation Stage 2 while general economy in Phase 4.

3. **Glove makers are pandemic-cycle dependent** — Don't fit standard Stovall; their cycle is health-event driven.

4. **REITs are interest-rate sensitive** — Phase 1 (rate cuts) is gold for REITs; Phase 3 (rate hikes) is punishing.

5. **USD/MYR matters more than US sectors** — KLSE exporters (tech, plantation, gloves) benefit from weak MYR regardless of phase. Importers (consumer, retail) prefer strong MYR.

## Part 6 — Rotation Timing (The Critical Edge)

**The market discounts the phase 4-6 months early.** This means:

- When economic data CONFIRMS Phase 2 → that's when retail traders enter
- By then, banks/tech have already moved 20-30%
- The Stovall trader entered 3-5 months earlier, on the FIRST signs of phase change

### Early Signal Identification

For each phase change, look for these LEADING signals (before economic data confirms):

#### Signal: Phase 1 → Phase 2 (Recovery beginning)
- KLCI breaks above 50-day MA on volume
- Sector rotation: banks and tech start outperforming
- USD/MYR begins weakening
- Foreign net selling slows or reverses
- McClellan Oscillator crosses above 0

#### Signal: Phase 2 → Phase 3 (Mid-cycle to late-cycle)
- Commodity prices accelerate (CPO, oil)
- BNM begins rate hike cycle
- Inflation prints accelerating
- Banking RS still strong but tech RS turning flat

#### Signal: Phase 3 → Phase 4 (Cycle peaking)
- Yield curve flattens to flat/inverted
- Commodity prices peak
- Industrial RS turns negative
- Defensive sectors (staples, utilities) start outperforming

#### Signal: Phase 4 → Phase 1 (Recession deepening)
- KLCI breaks decisively below 200-day MA
- BNM begins emergency rate cuts
- Foreign massively net selling
- REITs and gold outperforming everything
- McClellan deeply negative for extended periods

## Part 8 — Common Stovall Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Waiting for Phase Confirmation in News
By the time CNBC says "we're in recovery," banks are up 40%. Act on early SIGNALS, not late HEADLINES.

### Mistake 2: Rotating Too Fast
Phases last 6-18 months typically. Rotating every week defeats the purpose. Hold sector allocations for at least 1-2 months.

### Mistake 3: Holding Through Phase Change
If you're long energy and we transition to Phase 4 (early recession), commodity sectors will decline. Rotate OUT — don't hope.

### Mistake 4: Ignoring KLSE Quirks
The model works for the broad economy but plantation cycles, glove cycles, and tech cycles can diverge. Apply judgment.

### Mistake 5: 100% Rotation
Don't sell ALL banks when rotating from Phase 2 to Phase 3. Hold winners, trim losers, rotate incrementally.

### Mistake 6: Ignoring Stovall When VCP/Technical Says Otherwise
A perfect VCP in a Stage 2 stock in the WRONG sector for the phase has lower odds. Use Stovall as your top filter.

### Mistake 7: Predicting Phase Changes
You can't predict; you can identify signals. Stovall, like Weinstein, is reactive — wait for confirmation.

## Part 9 — Stovall + Weinstein + Marks (Triple Confirmation)

The most powerful macro setup is when all 3 frameworks align:

```
Stovall says: "We're in Phase 2 (Early Recovery)"
             → Banks should lead

Weinstein confirms: KLFIN Stage 2 ✓, RS rising ✓

Marks confirms: Cycle Position 3-4 ✓ (Early Recovery, best risk-reward)

= TRIPLE CONFIRMATION
Action: AGGRESSIVE long banks, full sizing, take cheat entries
```

When the 3 disagree:
- Stovall expects Phase 2 but Weinstein sees Stage 4 in target sectors → wait, don't deploy
- Marks position 7-8 but Stovall says Phase 2 → reduce sizing, be selective
- Marks position 3 but Weinstein sees no Stage 2 sectors → patient, sectors will follow macro

Triple alignment is the rare high-conviction setup. When you see it, act decisively.

## Part 10 — Stovall's Top Rules

1. **The market discounts the cycle.** Don't wait for headline confirmation; act on the early signal.

2. **Each phase has clear winners — and clear losers.** Trade the winners; avoid the losers entirely.

3. **Rotate incrementally, not abruptly.** Phases overlap; transitions are gradual.

4. **Honour the wheel direction.** The cycle moves 1→2→3→4→1. Don't expect it to skip.

5. **Phase 2 (Early Recovery) produces the best returns.** This is the highest-conviction phase to be aggressively long.

6. **Phase 1 (Full Recession) is for patience.** Defensive only; cash heavy; wait for Phase 2 signals.

7. **Combine with Weinstein for confirmation.** Stovall says where money SHOULD go; Weinstein confirms where money IS going.

8. **Update phase assessment monthly, not weekly.** The cycle moves slowly.

9. **Hold winners through phase transitions when momentum persists.** Mechanical rotation in the absence of weak signals = unnecessary cost.

10. **Stovall + valuation matters.** A leading sector in the right phase but at excessive valuations is still risky. Combine with [35_Stock_Valuation_Methods.md](35_Stock_Valuation_Methods.md).
