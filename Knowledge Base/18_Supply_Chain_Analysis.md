# Supply Chain Analysis — Every KLSE Sector

## Why Supply Chains Matter for Trading

A stock price moves BEFORE earnings are reported because smart money tracks the supply chain. If you know that upstream suppliers are booking record orders, downstream manufacturers will report strong earnings in 2–3 months. **Trade the signal before the earnings report.**

> "Invest in the picks and shovels, not just the gold miners."

---

## How to Use This File

For every stock you're considering, ask:
1. Who are the **upstream suppliers**? (Where do raw materials / components come from?)
2. Who are the **downstream customers**? (Who buys the finished product?)
3. What **macro factor** drives demand through the entire chain?
4. Where in the chain is the **highest margin** (pricing power)?
5. What **leading indicators** signal the cycle is turning?

## 1. Semiconductor Supply Chain

```
Raw Materials → Wafer Fabs → Assembly & Test (OSAT) → End Products
(Silicon, gases)  (TSMC, Intel)  (Inari, Unisem, MPI)  (Phones, PCs, Cars)
```

### Full Chain for KLSE Context

| Level | Global Players | KLSE Beneficiaries |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Design** | Qualcomm, MediaTek, Nvidia | — (none listed on KLSE) |
| **Wafer Fab** | TSMC, Samsung, Intel | — (none on KLSE at scale) |
| **Equipment & Materials** | AMAT, ASML, Merck | VITROX (inspection), FRONTKN (cleaning), UWC (precision parts), GREATEC |
| **OSAT** (packaging/testing) | ASE, Amkor | INARI, UNISEM, MPI, SAM |
| **Distribution** | Arrow, Avnet | — |
| **End demand** | Apple, Samsung, auto OEMs | All above stocks |

### Lead Indicators to Watch
| Indicator | Source | Signal |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| TSMC revenue guidance | TSMC quarterly call | 1–2 quarters ahead |
| US Semiconductor Book-to-Bill | SEMI.org monthly | >1.0 = growing orders |
| Nvidia/AMD capex plans | Quarterly earnings | AI chip demand |
| Apple iPhone shipment forecasts | IDC, Counterpoint | INARI revenue 6 months ahead |
| Auto chip lead times | Industry reports | Automotive semi demand |

### How to Trade the Semi Cycle
- **Buy**: When book-to-bill rises above 1.0 AND VITROX/INARI form VCP bases
- **Sell**: When book-to-bill falls below 1.0 AND TSMC cuts guidance
- **Key tell**: VITROX revenue usually leads sector by 1 quarter (equipment orders come before production ramp)

## 2. Plantation (Palm Oil) Supply Chain

```
Nursery → Planting → Harvesting (FFB) → Mill (CPO/PKO) → Refinery → Consumer/Industrial
           (5-7 yr)   KLSE planters      KLSE planters    IOI, KLK     F&B, biodiesel
```

### Full Chain

| Level | Players | KLSE Stocks |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Upstream: Plantation** | Grow oil palm trees, harvest FFB | KLK, IOICORP, GENP, SOP, UTDPLT |
| **Midstream: Milling** | Process FFB into CPO and PKO | Same as above (integrated) |
| **Downstream: Refining** | CPO → refined palm olein, stearin | KLK, IOICORP, SDGUTHRIE |
| **Oleochemicals** | CPO → soap, detergent, cosmetic raw materials | KLK, IOICORP |
| **End Demand** | F&B companies, biodiesel, India/China importers | PPB (Wilmar), NESTLE |

### Pricing Chain
```
CPO price → Downstream margins COMPRESS (refiners squeezed when CPO expensive)
CPO price → Upstream margins EXPAND (planters earn more per tonne)
```

**Trade implication**: When CPO price rises sharply, BUY pure upstream planters (GENP, SOP), not integrated players.

### Lead Indicators
| Indicator | Where to Find | What It Signals |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| MPOB Monthly Report | mpob.gov.my | Production, stocks, exports — published 10th of each month |
| Indonesia export tax | Indonesian govt | Higher tax = less competition for Malaysian CPO |
| Soybean crush spread | CBOT (CME) | When soy is expensive, palm is preferred substitute |
| India/China import data | Monthly trade statistics | Demand from world's top 2 importers |
| Weather (La Niña) | NOAA forecasts | Reduces FFB yield → tighter supply → higher CPO |

## 3. Construction & Infrastructure Supply Chain

```
Government Spending/Private Dev → Main Contractor → Sub-contractors → Materials Suppliers
(Budget allocation, GDV launches)  (GAMUDA, IJM, SUNCON)  (smaller builders)  (Cement, Steel, Sand)
```

### Full Chain

| Level | KLSE Stocks | Margin Profile |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| **Project owners** | MAHSING, SPSETIA (property) | High — land + development profit |
| **Main contractors** | GAMUDA, IJM, SUNCON, KERJAYA | Medium — 6–12% net margin |
| **Specialist sub-contractors** | Smaller listed firms | Lower |
| **Building materials** | MCEMENT, CMSB | Thin — commodity pricing |

### What Drives Construction Stocks
1. **Government budget allocations** — Budget night (October) is the single biggest catalyst
2. **MRT/LRT/highway contracts** — Project awards via MyProcurement portal
3. **Data centre construction** — Google, Microsoft, Oracle building in Johor/KL
4. **Property sales** — Developer GDV translates into construction orders within 12–18 months

### Lead Indicators
| Indicator | Signal |
|-----------|--------|
| Budget 2026/2027 allocation for infrastructure | Direct revenue pipeline |
| RAPID / Pengerang projects | Petronas downstream capex |
| JB-Singapore projects (RTS, SFZ) | Johor contractor demand |
| GAMUDA/IJM order book size | Published quarterly — growing book = buy |

### Trading Approach
- **Buy on contract award news**: Price surges when contract announced, then pulls back to VCP
- **Buy contractors before budget** (September/October): Anticipation of allocations
- **Sell on project completion**: Revenue visibility drops as project winds down
- **Watch cement prices**: Falling cement = margin expansion for contractors

## 4. Banking Supply Chain

```
Deposits (Savers) → Bank → Loans (Borrowers) → Interest Rate Spread = NIM
                           ↓
                    Investment Securities / Treasury
```

### Banking's "Raw Material" = Money

| Input | Equivalent | Drives |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Deposits (CASA) | Raw material | Funding cost (lower = better) |
| OPR (BNM rate) | Price of raw material | NIM (interest rate spread) |
| Loan demand | Volume sold | Revenue growth |
| Asset quality | Quality control | Credit costs (provisions) |

### What Banks Do With Money
1. **Retail lending**: Home loans (45% of portfolio), car loans, personal loans
2. **Corporate lending**: Business term loans, trade finance, project finance
3. **Treasury**: Bond investments, forex trading, derivatives

### Key Supply Chain Dependencies
- **Property market health** → Bank home loan quality (NPL)
- **SME health** → Bank corporate loan quality
- **Capital markets activity** → Investment banking fees (CIMB/MAYBANK)
- **Insurance penetration** → Bancassurance revenue (MAYBANK, PBBANK)

### Lead Indicators for Banking
| Indicator | Published By | Signal |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| BNM Monthly Statistical Bulletin | bnm.gov.my | Loan growth, CASA ratio, NPL |
| BNM Monetary Policy Decision | Every 6 weeks | OPR changes → NIM impact |
| Property transaction data | NAPIC | Forward loan demand |
| KLCI performance | Daily | Foreign flow into bank stocks |

## 5. Property Supply Chain

```
Land → Planning/Approval → Construction → Sales → Handover → After-market services
 ↑           ↑                  ↑           ↑
 (price)  (gov't policy)    (materials cost)  (mortgage rates)
```

### Full Chain

| Level | Players | Dependency |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Land** | Developers own landbank | Land cost determines margin |
| **Construction** | GAMUDA, IJM, SUNCON build for developers | Construction cost trend |
| **Financing** | Banks provide mortgages | OPR / mortgage rates |
| **Agents** | IQI, PropNex | Transaction velocity |
| **After-sales** | Maintenance, renovation | Recurring revenue |

### Key Metric: Unbilled Sales

```
Unbilled Sales = Contracts sold but not yet recognised as revenue
(Revenue locked in for next 2–3 years)
```

| Unbilled Sales vs Annual Revenue | Assessment |
|----------------------------------|------------|
| >3× | Excellent visibility; buy on dips |
| 2–3× | Good |
| 1–2× | Adequate |
| <1× | Revenue risk; caution |

## 6. Healthcare Supply Chain

```
Drug/Device Manufacturers → Distributors → Hospitals/Clinics → Patients
(Pfizer, Roche, Kossan/Hartalega for gloves)   (Pharmaniaga)    (IHH, KPJ)
```

### Glove Sub-Chain (KLSE-Specific)

```
NBR/Latex Rubber → Glove Manufacturer → Distributor → Hospital/Lab → Patient
(commodity price)  (TOPGLOV, KOSSAN, HARTA)
```

**Key cost**: 60–70% of glove production cost is raw material (NBR rubber, latex)
- NBR price rising → glove margins compress → sell TOPGLOV/KOSSAN
- NBR price falling → glove margins expand → buy

**Demand driver**: Global healthcare expenditure, pandemic alerts, lab/food industry glove standards

### Hospital Chain

```
Medical equipment/drugs → Hospital operator → Patients
(imported, USD-priced)      (IHH, KPJ)        (local + medical tourists)
```

**Key dependencies**:
- USD/MYR: Hospitals import 80%+ of equipment/drugs in USD
- MYR weakens → IHH/KPJ cost rises → margin pressure
- Medical tourism from Indonesia, India, Bangladesh → revenue upside

## 7. Technology/Telco Supply Chain

```
Infrastructure (towers, fibre) → Network operators → Enterprise/Consumer services
(MAXIS passive infra)            (MAXIS, CDB, AXIATA, TM)    (Data, voice, cloud)
```

### KLSE Telco Chain

| Level | KLSE Stock | Revenue Driver |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Tower infra** | — (passive infra REITs in development) | Tower rental |
| **Fibre network** | TM (UNIFI), TIME | Broadband subscribers |
| **Mobile network** | MAXIS, CDB, AXIATA (Celcom) | ARPU, data consumption |
| **Enterprise IT** | TM ONE, MAXIS Business | Corporate cloud/connectivity |
| **Data centre** | YTL POWER, GAMUDA (Ivory) | Colocation revenue |

**Key demand driver**: 5G rollout pace, AI/cloud adoption by enterprises, streaming consumption growth

### Data Centre Supply Chain (Emerging — High Priority for KLSE)

```
Power (TNB/YTLPOWR) → Cooling systems → Physical structure (SUNCON, GAMUDA) 
→ Network connectivity (TM) → Data centre operator → Cloud/AI customers (hyperscalers)
```

**KLSE beneficiaries of data centre boom**:
- **Power**: TENAGA, YTLPOWR (direct power supply contracts)
- **Construction**: GAMUDA, SUNCON (building the facilities)
- **Cooling/mechanical**: UWC, specialist engineers
- **Land**: Johor-based developers (UEMS, KSL)
- **Connectivity**: TM (fibre backbone)

**Lead indicator**: Hyperscaler (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) capex guidance → feeds into KLSE contractor/power orders 12–18 months later
