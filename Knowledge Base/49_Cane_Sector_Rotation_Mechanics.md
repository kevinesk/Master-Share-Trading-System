# Michael Cane — Sector Rotation Mechanics & Leading Indicators

> "Capital does not move randomly. It follows a predictable economic conveyor belt. Master the indicators that drive that belt, and you trade weeks ahead of the consensus."
> — Adapted from Michael S. Cane, *All About Sector Rotation*

While Stovall ([45_Stovall_Sector_Rotation_Model.md](45_Stovall_Sector_Rotation_Model.md)) provides the macro phase → sector mapping, Michael Cane's contribution is the **mechanical playbook of WHICH leading indicators predict WHICH sector rotations** — and in what chronological order.

The breakthrough: every sector has SPECIFIC economic indicators that telegraph its move 2-6 months ahead. Knowing these indicators lets you front-run institutional flows.

This file translates Cane's framework into a KLSE leading-indicator dashboard.

---

## Part 1 — The Three Layers of Sector Rotation

Cane identifies three time scales of sector rotation, each driven by different indicators:

### Layer 1: Long-Term Rotation (12-24 months) — Driven by the BUSINESS CYCLE

Same as Stovall — Phase 1, 2, 3, 4 of the economy. Indicators: GDP growth, employment, inflation, interest rates.

### Layer 2: Medium-Term Rotation (3-9 months) — Driven by MONETARY POLICY EXPECTATIONS

When the market anticipates a Fed/BNM pivot, sectors rotate 3-9 months ahead. Indicators: yield curve, futures-implied rate paths, central bank rhetoric.

### Layer 3: Short-Term Rotation (1-3 months) — Driven by SECTOR-SPECIFIC CATALYSTS

Earnings cycles, commodity prices, currency moves, industry events. Indicators: high-frequency data, commodity futures, FX, intermarket signals.

**The Cane edge**: most retail traders watch only Layer 1 (when the news headlines confirm it). Pros operate on Layer 2 (months ahead). Best traders combine all three.

## Part 3 — The Chronological Rotation Sequence

Here's the EXACT order sectors rotate, with leading indicators triggering each:

### From Phase 4 (Recession Bottom) → Phase 1 (Recovery)

```
Month 0-3:  Bonds rally (yields drop) — recession officially recognised
            → Indicator: yield curve uninverts, MGS rally
            
Month 3-6:  Defensive sectors lead (staples, utilities, healthcare)
            → Indicator: Defensive RS positive vs KLCI
            
Month 6-9:  Financials begin to recover
            → Indicator: BNM signals end of rate cuts
            → Loan growth turning up
            
Month 9-12: Technology and Consumer Discretionary lead
            → Indicator: SOX index rising, MIER Consumer Sentiment rising
            
Month 12+:  Broad small-cap rally begins
            → Indicator: KLSE breadth (% above MA50) > 60%
```

### From Phase 1 → Phase 2 (Mid-Expansion)

```
Month 0-3:  Tech rally matures — leadership rotates
            → Indicator: SOX makes new high but RS deteriorates
            
Month 3-6:  Industrials and Materials accelerate
            → Indicator: ISM Manufacturing PMI > 55, oil prices rising
            
Month 6-9:  Energy and Commodities lead
            → Indicator: Brent crude breakout, CPO rally
            
Month 9-12: Late-cycle inflation plays (gold-linked, mining)
            → Indicator: Gold rising, CPI accelerating
```

### From Phase 2 → Phase 3 (Late Expansion → Peak)

```
Month 0-3:  Energy and Commodities making climactic highs
            → Indicator: Brent +50% in 6 months, CPO above RM5500
            
Month 3-6:  Defensive sectors start outperforming
            → Indicator: Staples / Utilities RS turning positive
            → Yield curve flattening
            
Month 6-9:  Financials lag — banking RS turning negative
            → Indicator: Loan growth decelerating
            
Month 9-12: Broad market topping
            → Indicator: Distribution days, breadth deteriorating
```

### From Phase 3 → Phase 4 (Peak → Recession)

```
Month 0-3:  Cyclicals (industrials, energy) breaking down
            → Indicator: Sector RS turning sharply negative
            
Month 3-6:  Tech and Consumer Discretionary collapse
            → Indicator: SOX bear market, AEON crashing
            
Month 6-9:  Final defensives still holding
            → Indicator: Staples + Utilities the only green
            
Month 9-12: Capitulation — everything sells off
            → Indicator: VIX > 35, foreign selling extreme
```

Build this dashboard and check weekly. It's the single most powerful "early warning + opportunity" tool.

- BNM OPR (current + last change)

- Latest GDP YoY

### Block 3: Inflation (Layer 1-2)
- CPI YoY

- Brent crude
- CPO futures

### Block 5: Sentiment (Layer 3)
- MIER Consumer Sentiment Index
- KLSE Foreign Net Flows
- Margin Lending Outstanding (BNM)

### Where to Find KLSE Data
- **BNM website (bnm.gov.my)**: Monthly Highlights, Quarterly Bulletin
- **Statistics Malaysia (dosm.gov.my)**: GDP, CPI, manufacturing

- **NAPIC (napic.jpph.gov.my)**: Property transaction data
- **MPOB (mpob.gov.my)**: Palm oil inventory, exports

## Part 6 — The Cane Trading Rules

### Rule 1: Trade the Leading Indicator, Not the Confirmation

By the time GDP confirms recovery, the rally is 9-12 months old. By the time analysts upgrade, momentum is 3-6 months gone. Trade the LEADING signal.

### Rule 2: Match the Timeframe to the Indicator

- Layer 1 indicators (GDP, employment): position trades 6-12 months
- Layer 2 indicators (monetary): swing trades 2-6 months
- Layer 3 indicators (sector-specific): swing trades 1-3 months

### Rule 3: Multiple Indicators Confirm

A single bullish indicator can be noise. THREE aligned indicators for a sector = high conviction signal.

Example: Banks
- BNM in rate-hiking cycle ✓
- Loan growth accelerating ✓
- 10Y MGS yield rising ✓
- Three signals aligned = aggressive long banks

### Rule 4: When Indicators Diverge, Trust the Lower-Latency

If GDP says "recovery" but high-frequency PMI says "slowdown" → trust PMI. PMI leads GDP by 1-3 months.

### Rule 5: Sector RS is the Ultimate Confirmation

Even when leading indicators point bullish, if the sector RS line vs KLCI is falling, institutional money disagrees. Wait for RS confirmation.

### Rule 6: Combine with Cycle Position

A Cane "buy banks" signal in Phase 1 (Dalio/Stovall) = STRONG. The same signal in Phase 3 = much weaker. Indicators are context-dependent.

### Rule 7: Single Indicator, Single Trade Risk

Never bet the portfolio on ONE leading indicator. If a single indicator is wrong, the trade fails. With 3+ aligned, the trade has multiple paths to working.

### Rule 8: Patience for the Setup

Some indicator signals appear once a year, others once a quarter. Wait for the high-conviction setups; skip the marginal ones.

### Rule 9: Update Your Indicators Religiously

The dashboard must be UPDATED to be useful. Stale data = false signals. Sunday is dashboard day.

### Rule 10: Combine Cane with Morris

Cane gives you the MACRO signal for the sector. Morris ([50_Morris_Sector_Breadth_Indicators.md](50_Morris_Sector_Breadth_Indicators.md)) gives you the INTERNAL confirmation that stocks are being accumulated. Use both.

## Part 9 — The Cane Top Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Watching Lagging Indicators
GDP, unemployment, and earnings are LAGGING. By the time they confirm the trend, the trade is mostly over. Focus on leading.

### Mistake 2: Watching Headlines, Not Data
"BNM raises rates" headline is reaction. The yield curve, MPS language, and KLIBOR moved BEFORE the headline.

### Mistake 3: Single Indicator Conviction
One bullish data point doesn't make a trend. Wait for 3+.

### Mistake 4: Ignoring International Indicators
KLSE doesn't trade in a vacuum. US 10Y, USD, China PMI, regional flows all matter. Don't be KLSE-myopic.

### Mistake 5: Trading Against the Indicator
Sector RS is falling, but you're "sure" the sector will rally. Wrong. Trust the data; abandon the thesis.

### Mistake 6: Stale Dashboard
Data older than 2 weeks is too old for swing trading. Weekly refresh minimum.

### Mistake 7: Confusing Layers
Using a Layer 1 indicator (long-term GDP) to time a Layer 3 trade (3-week swing) = wrong tool.
