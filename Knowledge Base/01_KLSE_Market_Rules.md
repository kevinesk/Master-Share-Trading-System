# KLSE Market Rules & Structure

## Trading Hours (Bursa Malaysia — MYT / UTC+8)
| Session       | Time              | Notes                          |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------------------------|
| Pre-open      | 08:30 – 09:00     | Order matching, no trades      |
| Morning       | 09:00 – 12:30     | Continuous trading             |
| Lunch break   | 12:30 – 14:00     | No trades                      |
| Afternoon     | 14:00 – 17:00     | Continuous trading             |
| Pre-close     | 17:00 – 17:08     | Closing call auction           |
| Off-hours     | 17:08 – next day  | No trades                      |

**Market closed:** Weekends + Malaysian public holidays (Wesak, Eid, CNY, Merdeka, etc.)

---

## Lot Size & Minimum Trade
- **1 lot = 100 shares**
- Minimum trade = 1 lot (100 shares)
- Odd lots (< 100 shares) can be traded on the Odd Lot Market at wider spreads

## Price Tick Sizes
| Share Price (RM)  | Tick Size (RM) |
|-------------------|----------------|
| < 0.50            | 0.005          |

| 1.00 – 2.99       | 0.005          |

---

## Settlement & Payment
- **T+2 settlement**: Trade on Monday → pay/receive by Wednesday
- **Contra trades**: Buy then sell (or sell then buy) the same stock within T+2 without full cash — only the net profit/loss settles. High risk — losses are amplified.
- **Forced selling**: Broker force-sells if margin call not met by T+2

---

## Trading Fees & Costs
| Fee                   | Rate                          |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Brokerage (online)    | 0.1% – 0.42% min RM8         |
| Clearing fee          | 0.03% (capped RM1,000/day)    |
| Stamp duty            | 0.15% (capped RM200/contract) |
| Bursa fee             | 0.003%                        |
| **Total round trip**  | ~0.3% – 0.6% per side        |

> **Trading tip:** On a RM10,000 trade, total round-trip cost ≈ RM60–120. Position must move >1% just to break even.

---

## Circuit Breakers & Price Limits
- **Individual stock**: No hard limit up/down. But trades outside 20% of last done may be queried.
- **Market-wide**: Trading halts if KLCI drops ≥15% intraday (rarely triggered)
- **Volatility auction**: If price moves >10% from reference, enters a 5-minute call auction

---

## Short Selling Rules
- **Approved** for Regulated Short Selling (RSS) on selected stocks only
- Must borrow shares before selling — not free borrowing
- Short selling **not allowed** for retail investors without RSS licence
- Most KLSE retail traders are **long-only**

---

## Foreign Ownership
- Foreign investors can buy most Bursa stocks freely
- Some sectors have foreign ownership limits: media (49%), banking (30% per foreign individual), broadcasting
- Foreign flows heavily influence large-cap stocks (KLCI 30)

---

## Key Market Participants
| Participant        | Behaviour Impact                                 |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Foreign funds      | Drive KLCI; USD/MYR and global risk-on/off key  |
| EPF / PNB          | Anchor buyers in large caps; reduce volatility  |
| Retail traders     | Dominant in mid/small cap; sentiment-driven     |
| Proprietary desks  | Follow technical breaks; provide liquidity      |

---

## Bursa Account Types
- **CDS account**: Central Depository System — holds your shares electronically
- **Cash account**: Buy with available cash only; standard retail
- **Margin account**: Borrow up to 50–70% of portfolio value; amplifies gains AND losses

---

## Key Dates Every Trader Must Monitor
- **Quarterly results**: Companies report EPS within 2 months of quarter-end
- **Ex-dividend date**: Buy BEFORE ex-date to receive dividend
- **AGM announcements**: May signal corporate actions
- **Index review**: KLCI & MID70 reviewed semi-annually (June and December)
- **BNM Monetary Policy**: 6 meetings per year — rate changes impact banks, REITs
