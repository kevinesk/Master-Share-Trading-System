# Dual-Style Playbook — Momentum + Yield Across the Cycle

> "When market internals are healthy, your momentum setups fund your lifestyle and build your capital. When internals deteriorate, your defensive yield assets protect that capital and pay you a high premium to wait for the next cycle."

This is the **portfolio allocation strategy** layer of your system. It sits between [TOP_DOWN_FRAMEWORK.md](TOP_DOWN_FRAMEWORK.md) (which tells you the cycle phase) and [MASTER_DECISION_TREE.md](MASTER_DECISION_TREE.md) (which executes individual trades).

The framework: run **TWO portfolios simultaneously** — a momentum/growth bucket and a defensive/yield bucket. Their relative sizing changes with the macro cycle. When one bucket is underperforming, the other is your shelter and income source.

---

## Part 1 — The Core Idea: Two Buckets, One Cycle

### Why Two Buckets?

Most retail traders run a single style:
- Pure momentum → great in bulls, devastating in bears
- Pure dividend/yield → safe but misses 80% of compounded returns
- Pure value → can underperform for years

The dual-style trader runs BOTH:
- **Bucket A (Momentum/Growth)**: VCP breakouts, Fast Growers, high-velocity swing trades
- **Bucket B (Defensive/Yield)**: REITs, utilities, consumer staples, dividend cyclicals

The buckets are **inversely correlated across the cycle** — when one is at full deployment, the other is at minimum, and vice versa. The portfolio breathes with the cycle.

### The Macro Clock

```
                  PHASE 2: Mid-to-Late Expansion
                  (Rising rates, high inflation)
                       ╱              ╲
                     ╱                  ╲
                   ╱                      ╲
   PHASE 1: Trough & Early Recovery       PHASE 3: Peak to Recession
   (Cutting rates, recovery starting)     (Slowing, contracting)
                   ╲                      ╱
                     ╲                  ╱
                       ╲              ╱
                  PHASE 4: Recession Bottom
                  (Forced selling, capitulation)
```

The macro clock dictates portfolio weighting. Identify the phase → set the dual-bucket weights → execute.

## Part 2 — Phase 1: Trough & Early Recovery

**Macro signal**: Central bank cutting rates aggressively. Liquidity flooding the system. Credit cheaper than 12 months ago. Stovall Phase 1→2. Marks cycle position 2-4.

**KLSE signal**: BNM has cut OPR. KLCI breaking above 50-day MA. USD/MYR stabilising/weakening. Foreign net buying returning. McClellan above 0.

### Bucket A (Momentum/Growth): AGGRESSIVE MAX EXPOSURE 🚀

**This is the golden window for momentum trading.**

```
Action: Scale up. Maximum position sizes on VCP breakouts.
        Take full positions on Stage 2 entries.
        Use cheat entries on tight VCPs.
        4-6 active positions.
        70-85% of trading capital deployed.
```

**Hunting grounds**:
- Technology / Semiconductors (VITROX, INARI, FRONTKN, MPI, GTRONIC)
- Small-caps with growth catalysts
- Consumer Discretionary (AEON, MR DIY, BJFOOD)
- Construction (early infrastructure spending — GAMUDA, IJM, SUNCON)
- Banks (credit growth resumes — MAYBANK, CIMB, RHB)

**Why it works**: Low rates make growth stocks worth more (lower discount rate on future earnings). Risk appetite is rising from oversold lows. Institutional money flows aggressively into high-beta.

**Failure rate**: Very low. Breakouts have 60-70% follow-through. "Eggs" (failed breakouts) are rare in this phase.

### Bucket B (Defensive/Yield): REIT REBOUND SELECTION

```
Action: Hold and accumulate.
        Position sizing 15-30% of total portfolio.
        Focus on rate-sensitive REITs.
```

**Hunting grounds**:
- Residential REITs (Sunway REIT, Pavilion REIT residential exposure)
- Commercial REITs (IGB REIT, KLCC, Pavilion KL Mall)
- Industrial REITs (Axis REIT — logistics/warehouses)

**Why it works**: Falling interest rates reduce REIT debt-servicing costs AND make their 5-7% distributions look attractive vs falling bond yields. REIT NAV expands as cap rates compress.

**Avoid**: Healthcare/utilities — they're defensive plays that lag in recoveries.

### Phase 1 Portfolio Allocation

```
Bucket A (Momentum/Growth):     70-85%
Bucket B (Defensive/Yield):     15-25%  
Cash:                            0-10%
```

## Part 3 — Phase 2: Mid-to-Late Expansion

**Macro signal**: Strong GDP growth. Central bank started raising rates. Inflation rising. Yield curve flattening.

**KLSE signal**: KLCI near or making new highs. BNM has raised OPR 1-2 times. Inflation prints accelerating. CPO/oil at multi-year highs.

### Bucket A (Momentum/Growth): HIGH SELECTIVITY ⚠️

**The broad rally splinters. Easy money has been made. Discipline required.**

```
Action: Reduce broad exposure.
        Take only A+ setups (9-10 on confluence checklist).
        Tighter stops (10-day or 21-day EMA, not 50-day).
        Take partial profits earlier (+15-20%).
        3-4 active positions.
        50-65% of trading capital deployed.
```

**Hunting grounds shift** — away from speculative tech, toward:
- **Materials** (PCHEM, LCT, plantation — KLK, IOI, SIME)
- **Industrials** (capex beneficiaries — HEXTAR, MISC)
- **Energy / Oil & Gas** (PETDAG, PETRONAS, DIALOG)
- **Late-cycle commodity plays** (cement — YTL, gold-linked names)

**Avoid**:
- High-multiple tech (P/E > 30, vulnerable to rate compression)
- Pure consumer discretionary (consumer slowing)
- Long-duration growth stories

**Why VCPs become choppy**: Institutions are rotating, not unanimously buying. Many bases fail. Breakouts pull back more often. The "easy" 70% hit rate of Phase 1 drops to 50-55%.

### Bucket B (Defensive/Yield): THE DEFENSIVE SHIFT

```
Action: Lighten rate-sensitive REITs (they face headwinds from rising rates).
        Rotate into cash-rich cyclical dividend payers.
        Position sizing 25-40% of total portfolio.
```

**Hunting grounds**:
- Cash-rich cyclicals at peak earnings (special dividends often)
- Banking dividends (PBBANK, MAYBANK — benefit from higher rates)
- Selected manufacturing/automotive with strong balance sheets (UMW, MBM)
- Plantation at peak CPO (TSH, GENP — special dividends from windfall profits)

**Reduce**:
- Long-duration REITs (commercial property cap rates expanding hurts NAV)
- Bond-proxy utilities (compete with rising bond yields)

**Why it works**: Cash-rich cyclicals at the cycle peak often announce special dividends from windfall earnings. Banks earn more from higher net interest margins. Both pay generously while you wait for the slowdown.

### Phase 2 Portfolio Allocation

```
Bucket A (Momentum/Growth):     50-65%
Bucket B (Defensive/Yield):     25-40%
Cash:                            5-15%
```

## Part 4 — Phase 3: Peak to Recession

**Macro signal**: Yield curve inverted or flat. PMI below 50. Unemployment rising. Central bank done hiking; may start cutting.

**KLSE signal**: KLCI below 50-day MA. Distribution days clustering (5+ in 25 sessions). Foreign net selling persistent. USD/MYR strengthening. McClellan deeply negative.

### Bucket A (Momentum/Growth): CAPITAL PRESERVATION 🛡️

**Cash is king. Stop fighting the tape.**

```
Action: NO new long entries.
        Take all profits aggressively (1/2 at +15%, rest with tight trailing stop).
        Move all stops to breakeven on remaining positions.
        Maximum 1-2 active positions (highest conviction only).
        15-30% of trading capital deployed maximum.
```

**Why momentum fails here**:
- Institutions are DISTRIBUTING, not accumulating
- Breakouts fail at 65-75% rate (the "egg")
- Even perfect setups give up gains within days
- Volume on rallies is weak (no follow-through)

**Common trap**: "But the setup looks textbook." It does. They all do. The macro is against you. **Step away.**

**Optional**: Micro-sized scalps only — intraday or 1-2 day swings on extreme oversold bounces. Quick in, quick out. Treat as entertainment, not capital growth.

### Bucket B (Defensive/Yield): AGGRESSIVE ACCUMULATION 💎

**This is where the defensive bucket EARNS ITS KEEP.**

```
Action: Scale UP aggressively.
        Position sizing 50-70% of total portfolio.
        Buy on every meaningful correction.
        Lock in high yields at discounted valuations.
```

**Hunting grounds** — bulletproof defensive yield:
- **Healthcare REITs** (Al-Aqar Healthcare REIT — hospitals)
- **Logistics / Warehouse REITs** (Axis REIT)
- **Utilities** (TENAGA, YTL POWER, GAS MALAYSIA)
- **Consumer Staples** (NESTLE, F&N, DLADY, CARLSBG)
- **Telecoms** (MAXIS, CDB — recurring subscription revenue)
- **Gold / safe-haven plays**

**Why this is the goldmine of the cycle**:
- Defensive sectors' EARNINGS stay flat (people still buy Milo, take electricity, see doctors)
- But their STOCK PRICES correct with the broader market
- Result: dividend YIELD spikes (same dollar payment, lower price = higher %)
- You're buying 6-8% yields on companies with bulletproof cash flows
- When the cycle turns, you keep the yield AND get capital appreciation

**KLSE example math**:
- TENAGA pays ~RM0.50 DPS annually
- At RM12.00 → yield 4.2% (Phase 1 level)
- At RM9.00 (after market correction) → yield 5.6% — locked in
- When market recovers, you have 5.6% yield + capital gain

### Phase 3 Portfolio Allocation

```
Bucket A (Momentum/Growth):     15-30%  (or 0% if disciplined)
Bucket B (Defensive/Yield):     50-70%
Cash:                            15-30%  (dry powder for accumulation)
```

## Part 5 — Phase 4: Recession Bottom (Bonus Phase)

**Macro signal**: Forced selling. Capitulation. VIX > 30. Central bank emergency easing.

**KLSE signal**: KLCI down 30%+ from peak. Daily price moves > 3% common. Foreign selling at multi-year highs. Sentiment surveys at extreme fear.

### Bucket A (Momentum/Growth): PREPARE TO POUNCE

```
Action: Still no major entries — wait for confirmation.
        BUT: Begin building watchlists of future leaders.
        Identify Stage 1 sectors that will become Stage 2.
        Mark VCP candidates that are still forming.
        Test entries with 25% of normal size on emerging strength.
```

### Bucket B (Defensive/Yield): MAXIMUM ACCUMULATION + REIT POSITIONING

```
Action: Maximum defensive deployment.
        ALSO: Begin layering into rate-sensitive REITs.
        Why? Central bank cuts are coming. REITs will benefit first.
```

**The Phase 4 → 1 transition** is the most lucrative single moment in the cycle. Be positioned BEFORE the turn, not after.

### Phase 4 Portfolio Allocation

```
Bucket A (Momentum/Growth):     5-15%   (toe-dip starter positions)
Bucket B (Defensive/Yield):     60-75%  (yield + rate-cut beneficiaries)
Cash:                            15-30%  (final dry powder before recovery)
```

| Macro Metric | Environment | Bucket A: Momentum/Growth | Bucket B: Yield/REIT |
|--------------|-------------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| **Falling rates + low inflation** | Loose Liquidity (Early Bull) | **SCALE UP**: max position sizes on VCP breakouts and Stage 2 uptrends | **HOLD/ACCUMULATE**: cyclical/industrial REITs benefit from low borrowing costs |
| **Rising rates + high inflation** | Tightening (Late Bull) | **SCALE DOWN**: tighter risk controls; focus only on commodity/industrial themes | **ROTATE**: lighten rate-sensitive REITs; buy high-cash-flow cyclical dividend stocks |
| **Peaking rates + slowing GDP** | Defensive (Bear Market) | **SIT ON HANDS**: cash or micro swings only | **SCALE UP**: lock in high yields on healthcare & staples REITs |
| **Cutting rates + recovery starting** | Recovery (Cycle Turn) | **AGGRESSIVE**: full deployment on emerging leaders | **BRIDGE**: keep yield, but tilt toward rate-cut beneficiaries |

## Part 10 — The 10 Dual-Style Commandments

1. **Never run only one bucket.** Pure momentum traders die in bear markets. Pure yield investors miss the wealth compounding of bull markets.

2. **The buckets are inversely sized across the cycle.** If both are full at the same time, you're not adjusting to macro.

3. **Don't fight Phase 3 in Bucket A.** No matter how perfect a setup looks, momentum dies in Phase 3. Walk away.

4. **Buy REITs when their yields are FAT, not when they're sexy.** A 7% yield in Phase 3 beats a 4.5% yield in Phase 1 — but you must have cash ready when nobody else wants them.

5. **Don't compound dividends from Bucket B into Bucket A in Phase 3.** Let dividends accumulate in cash. Deploy into Bucket A when Phase 1 returns.

6. **The transition is the trade.** The most profitable single moment is Phase 3 → Phase 1 transition. Be positioned EARLY in Bucket B; rotate FAST into Bucket A on confirmation.

7. **Special dividends are gifts — but exit the underlying stock after collection.** Cyclical companies pay special dividends at their peak. Take the dividend, exit the stock, don't hold for the next cycle.

8. **Trust the cycle, not the news.** "Healthcare looks expensive" — yes, because we're approaching Phase 3 and rotation has started. The valuation reflects the next phase, not the current one.

9. **Yields you DON'T trade are still profitable.** A REIT yielding 6.5% generates 6.5% even if the stock price does nothing. Don't trade out of yield positions chasing 2% capital moves.

10. **The dual-style portfolio is a marathon.** The best dual-style traders compound 12-18% annually across full cycles, not 60% in one bull then -50% in one bear.

## Part 11 — The Ultimate Swing-Income Rule (Print and Frame)

> **When market internals are healthy, your momentum setups fund your lifestyle and build your capital. When market internals deteriorate, your defensive yield assets protect that capital and pay you a high premium to wait patiently for the next cycle to begin.**

This is the entire dual-style philosophy in one sentence.

- Bucket A = active wealth building (when conditions allow)
- Bucket B = passive wealth protection AND income (always)

Together, they make sure you're paid in every phase of the cycle — never holding pure cash with zero return, never trapped fighting an adverse macro.

### Macro Snapshot
- BNM cut OPR twice in last 6 months
- KLCI back above 50-day MA
- USD/MYR weakening (foreign flows in)
- Phase 1 confirmed
- Marks cycle position: 4 (early recovery, doubt remains)

### Portfolio Setup (RM 100,000 example)

**Bucket A (Momentum) — 75%**
- VITROX VCP breakout: RM 18,000 (18%)

**Bucket B (Yield) — 20%**
- Sunway REIT: RM 7,000 (7%) — cyclical recovery
- Axis REIT: RM 7,000 (7%) — industrial logistics

This is the discipline: the macro changes, the weights change, the holdings change. You always have something working.
