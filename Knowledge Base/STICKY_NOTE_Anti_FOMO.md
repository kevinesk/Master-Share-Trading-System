# 🛑 BEFORE YOU CLICK BUY — READ THIS

---

## THE 5 TRUTHS

1. Anything can happen.
2. I don't need to know what's next to make money.
3. Wins and losses are randomly distributed within my edge.
4. My edge is just a probability — not a prediction.
5. Every moment in the market is unique.

---

## THE 3-STEP FOMO INTERRUPT

**1. NAME IT** → Say out loud: *"This is FOMO. This is not analysis."*

**2. APPLY THE 2-DAY RULE** → If it wasn't on yesterday's watchlist, wait 2 days.

**3. ASK THE KILLER QUESTION:**

> *"If I had bought this stock at yesterday's close, would I be selling it today at this price?"*

If yes → **DO NOT BUY.** You'd be buying what someone else is selling.

---

## THE STOP PROTOCOL (when panicking mid-trade)

- **S** — Stop. Hands off keyboard.
- **T** — Take 3 breaths (4 sec in, 6 sec out)
- **O** — Observe. What is the emotion? What is the trigger?
- **P** — Proceed only with the rule. What does my system say?

---

## THE 7 COMMANDMENTS

1. Missing a trade costs **0%**. Chasing one can cost **10%**.
2. The 2-day rule: if it wasn't on yesterday's watchlist, **wait**.
3. Any single trade is meaningless. The next 100 trades are everything.
4. I am not predicting. I am responding to my system.
5. If I'm panicking, my size is too big. **Cut it.**
6. The market opens again tomorrow. Today is not the only day.
7. **Process before P&L. Always.**

---

## IDENTITY STATEMENTS (read every morning)

- I am a **process** trader, not a prediction trader.
- I am someone who **cuts losses without hesitation**.
- I am someone who can **pass on a trade without regret**.
- I am someone who thinks in **probabilities**, not certainties.
- I trade the same whether last trade won or lost.

---

## RED FLAGS — STEP AWAY FROM THE SCREEN

- 🚩 I MUST act NOW
- 🚩 Friends are messaging about this stock
- 🚩 I'm thinking about money I could make, not the setup
- 🚩 I'm trying to "make back" a previous loss
- 🚩 The stock is up >15% today
- 🚩 I can't articulate the setup in one sentence

---

> **Process before P&L. The market opens again tomorrow.**
