# Wyckoff Method & Volume Spread Analysis (VSA)

## What is Wyckoff?

Richard Wyckoff (1873–1934) was a professional tape reader who reverse-engineered how large institutions (the "Composite Man") accumulate and distribute stocks. His method is the foundation of VSA (Volume Spread Analysis).

**Core insight**: Price moves because of an imbalance between supply and demand. Volume is the fuel. The spread (candle size) tells you direction. Together they reveal what institutions are doing.

---

## The Composite Man (Smart Money)

Think of the market as controlled by one invisible participant — the Composite Man — who:
1. **Accumulates** stock quietly (buying cheap before a rally)
2. **Marks up** price (lets the public see the move)
3. **Distributes** stock to the public at the top (selling into strength)
4. **Marks down** price (public panics, CM buys again)

Your job: **Identify Phase 2 (markup) and ride it. Avoid Phase 3 (distribution).**

## The Four Wyckoff Phases

### Phase 1 — Accumulation (Smart Money Buying)

```
        SC   AR        ST      Spring
         \   /\        /\      /\
__________\/  \________/\____/  \_______  ← Trading Range
                \______/
                   Creek (resistance)
```

Key events:
| Event | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **PS** (Preliminary Support) | First support appears; buying begins |
| **SC** (Selling Climax) | Heavy volume, sharp down, then recovery — panic sellers exhausted |
| **AR** (Automatic Rally) | Price bounces — establishes top of range |
| **ST** (Secondary Test) | Price retests SC lows on LOWER volume — confirms support |
| **Spring** | Final shakeout below support on LOW volume — then strong recovery. **Best entry point.** |
| **SOS** (Sign of Strength) | Strong price advance on high volume after Spring |
| **LPS** (Last Point of Support) | Pullback to former resistance (now support) on low volume — **final entry before markup** |

**KLSE application**: Look for Spring + SOS + LPS on weekly chart. These set up the best low-risk entries.

---

### Phase 2 — Markup (Ride This Phase)

- Price breaks above the accumulation range (Creek)
- Strong advances on high volume
- Pullbacks are shallow, on low volume
- EMA50 trending up, price above all major EMAs
- This is the **Stage 2 uptrend** — buy and hold

---

### Phase 3 — Distribution (Smart Money Selling)

```
___________________________________
                                   \
UT   \    LPSY         LPSY         \
 \    \___/\_____/\____/\____________\
  \__/   Buying Climax  SOW
```

Key events:
| Event | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **BC** (Buying Climax) | Wide spread up on HUGE volume — institutions selling into demand |
| **AR** (Automatic Reaction) | Sharp decline after BC |
| **UT** (Upthrust) | Price pops above resistance on high volume, then closes back inside — **trap for buyers** |
| **SOW** (Sign of Weakness) | Down move on high volume — distribution accelerating |
| **LPSY** (Last Point of Supply) | Rally on low volume, fails — **final exit opportunity** |

**KLSE rule**: If you see a Buying Climax + Upthrust, TAKE PROFITS. Do not wait.

---

### Phase 4 — Markdown (Stage 4 Downtrend)

- Price breaks below distribution range
- Do not buy. Do not average down. Go to cash.

## VSA — Reading Bar by Bar

Volume Spread Analysis condenses Wyckoff into 3 elements:
1. **Spread** (candle range — High minus Low)
2. **Close position** (where did price close relative to its range?)
3. **Volume** (how much activity?)

### VSA Combinations Table

| Spread | Close | Volume | Interpretation |
|--------|-------|--------|----------------|
| Wide up | High | High | Demand in control — bullish |
| Wide up | High | Low | **No demand** — rally likely to fail |
| Wide up | Low | High | **Upthrust** — institutions selling into rally |
| Narrow | Middle | High | **Churning** — smart money distributing |
| Wide down | Low | High | Supply in control — bearish |
| Wide down | Low | Low | **No supply** — selling exhausting — bullish |
| Narrow down | Middle | Low | **Test** — supply drying up — bullish |

### The 3 Most Important VSA Signals for KLSE

**1. No Supply (Bullish — Best Setup)**
- Narrow down candle (small range)
- Closes in upper half
- LOW volume
- Meaning: Sellers have left the market. Any buying tips price up.
- **Action**: Buy on the next up candle with increasing volume

**2. No Demand (Bearish Warning)**
- Narrow up candle
- Closes in lower half or middle
- LOW volume
- Meaning: Buyers are not interested in pushing price higher
- **Action**: Do not buy. If holding, tighten stop.

**3. Stopping Volume / Selling Climax**
- Wide down candle
- Closes in upper half (buyers step in)
- VERY HIGH volume
- Meaning: Panic selling absorbed by institutions
- **Action**: Watch for confirmation (reduced volume on next down bar). Entry coming.

## Practical KLSE Scanning for Wyckoff

**Weekly chart scan** (run on Sunday):
1. Find stocks that have been in a tight range for 8–26 weeks (accumulation)
2. Check: Did the stock recently dip below prior support and recover? (Spring)
3. Check: Is volume lower on down weeks than up weeks? (Accumulation)
4. Check: Has price broken above the range on volume? (SOS — markup starting)

**Daily chart confirmation**:
1. Look for LPS on daily — pullback to former breakout level on low volume
2. VSA: Look for "No Supply" bars (narrow down, low volume)
3. Buy on the first strong up day after No Supply

## Wyckoff Springs — Best Low-Risk Entry on KLSE

The Spring is the single best Wyckoff entry because:
- You buy at the lowest risk point (near the range low)
- Stop goes just below the Spring low (very tight)
- Reward is the full markup phase

**How to identify a valid Spring**:
1. Stock has been in a trading range for ≥8 weeks
2. Price dips below prior support (the "Creek") briefly
3. Volume on the break is LOWER than on prior down bars (weak sellers)
4. Price recovers BACK ABOVE support within 1–3 days
5. The next up move has wide spread + high volume (SOS)

**Invalid Spring** (do not buy):
- Volume on the break is very high (panic, not shakeout)
- Price fails to recover quickly
- Price closes below support repeatedly

## Common Wyckoff Mistakes

| Mistake | The Fix |
|---------|---------|
| Buying in Phase 3 (Distribution) thinking it's Phase 1 | Check: Is the stock up >50% already? Likely distributing. |
| Mistaking a Spring for a breakdown | Require: Volume lower on break than on prior declines |
| Exiting during Phase 2 markup pullbacks | Wait for VSA "No Supply" + LPS before worrying |
| Ignoring the broad market Wyckoff phase | KLCI must also be in markup (Phase 2) for individual stocks to work |
